📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Match pairs of identical icons. Flip two cards at a time — if they match, they stay open! Clear all pairs to win each stage.

  • 🐾 Stage 1: 4x4 grid (8 pairs) — Animal faces
  • 🥦 Stage 2: 6x6 grid (18 pairs) — Veggies (big icons)
  • 🚂 Stage 3: 6x6 grid (18 pairs) — Transport (big icons)
  • 🔄 Fewer moves = better memory!
  • 🏆 Complete all three stages for full victory

✨ Pro tip: Click on a card to reveal it, remember positions, and match. Use "NEW GAME" to reshuffle current stage.
