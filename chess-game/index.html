📜 How to Play

✨ Golden Chess follows standard chess rules with a luxurious look. Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move.

  • ♙♟ Pawn: Forward 1 or 2 (first move), capture diagonally.
  • ♖♜ Rook: Horizontal / vertical any distance.
  • ♘♞ Knight: L-shape (2+1), jumps over pieces.
  • ♗♝ Bishop: Diagonal any distance.
  • ♕♛ Queen: Rook + Bishop combined.
  • ♔♚ King: One step any direction. Cannot move into check.
🛡️ Check & Checkmate
If your King is under attack, you must move out of check. No moves left = Checkmate → you lose.
💎 Precious Pieces
White pieces shine like gold, Black pieces have gemstone glow. Valid moves show golden ✨ sparkles.

🎯 Goal: Capture the opponent's King or trap it (checkmate). The game announces winner automatically.
