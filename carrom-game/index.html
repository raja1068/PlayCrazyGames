🎯📖 HOW TO PLAY CARROM
🎮 AIM & SHOOT
Move mouse/finger to rotate arrow. Click & drag BACKWARDS from striker to set power, then release to shoot! Green arrow means pocket chance.
👑 QUEEN RULE
Red queen must be pocketed AND covered by pocketing your own coin in same turn. If opponent pockets queen first, they must cover.
⚠️ FOUL RULE
If striker goes in pocket: FOUL! One of YOUR coins is returned to centre circle. Turn passes. Also, if you pocket opponent's coin without covering queen → foul.
✨ EXTRA TURN
Pocket your own coin (+ cover queen) grants extra turn. Pocketing opponent loses turn. First to pocket all 9 coins + queen wins.
🖱️ DRAG STRIKER
Drag striker left/right along baseline before shot. Click manual turn buttons to switch turn if needed (hot seat).
🏆 WIN CONDITION
Clear all your 9 coins (white/black) + queen covered. Score updates automatically. Press NEW MATCH to restart.
