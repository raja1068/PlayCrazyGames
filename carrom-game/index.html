🎯📖 HOW TO PLAY
🎮 AIM & SHOOT
Drag back from striker to set power, release to shoot. Green arrow = pocket chance.
🔢 SCORING SYSTEM
All 19 coins numbered 1 to 19. Pocket a coin → ADD its number to your score instantly.
♾️ NO MOVE LIMIT
Players take turns until all coins are pocketed. Game ends when board is empty.
✨ TURN ALTERNATION
After your shot (pocket or miss), turn passes to opponent.
🏆 WINNER
When all coins are pocketed, player with higher total score wins. Splash banner appears!
🖱️ STRIKER CONTROL
Drag striker left/right along baseline. Pull back and release to strike.
