❌ Tic-Tac-Toe ⭕

📜 How to Play 🎯

Classic Duel — simple & strategic! Two players take turns marking the spaces in a 3×3 grid.

  • ✅ Player X goes first. Click any empty cell to place your mark.
  • ✅ Player O follows. The goal: get three of your marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).
  • ✅ If all 9 cells are filled and no one has 3 in a row, the game ends in a draw.
  • ✅ Press ⟳ Reset Game to start a fresh match anytime.
  • ✅ Use ⬅ Back to Hub to return to main menu.
⭐ Tip: ❌ X wins = green ⭕ O wins = red 🧠 plan ahead!
