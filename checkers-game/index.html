⬛ ROYAL CHECKERS 👑

📜 HOW TO PLAY 🎲
🎯 Click piece → valid green square
⚡ Jump over enemy = capture!
👑 Reach last row → KING (moves backward)
🏆 Capture all enemy pieces to win
🔄 Multiple jumps possible in one turn
🔴 Red's Turn
🔴 Red: 12
⚫ Black: 12
✨ TIP: You MUST capture if a jump is available. Kings move diagonally any direction.
🧠 After a capture, you may continue capturing with the same piece (multi-jump)!
